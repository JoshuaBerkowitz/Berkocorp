# Berkocorp brand guide

## Brand idea

**Built for the long horizon.**

Berkocorp should feel like an owner: patient, direct, curious, and comfortable being early. The identity pairs institutional clarity with the warmth of a multigenerational family office.

The architectural **B** is divided by a precise horizon line. Its stable outer structure represents permanence; the inner copper arch represents continuity, stewardship, and value compounding across generations.

## Verbal identity

- Positioning line: **Built for the long horizon.**
- Supporting line: **Patient capital. Clear conviction.**
- Voice: informed, candid, concise, lightly witty.
- Prefer: concrete language, plain-English judgment, useful specifics.
- Avoid: inflated finance language, institutional theatre, vague claims, trend-chasing.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Evergreen | `#0E2A23` |
| Dark ground | Deep Evergreen | `#061712` |
| Accent | Oxidized Copper | `#C66A3D` |
| Light ground | Warm Ivory | `#F3EBDD` |
| Bright ground | Bright Ivory | `#FBF7EF` |
| Secondary | Mineral Sage | `#7C9188` |
| Border / neutral | Stone | `#DED5C5` |

Evergreen and ivory should dominate. Copper is an accent, not a background default. Sage is reserved for secondary information and quiet data elements.

## Typography

- Display and editorial headlines: Iowan Old Style, then Baskerville or Times New Roman.
- Navigation, body, and UI: Avenir Next, then Avenir or Segoe UI.
- Data and annotations: SFMono Regular, then Consolas or Menlo.

Use generous space and a restrained size hierarchy. The wordmark is uppercase with measured tracking; body copy remains sentence case.

## Logo use

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of the symbol's vertical spine.
- Use the full horizontal lockup above 180 px wide. Use the standalone mark below that width.
- Minimum digital sizes: 180 px for the lockup, 24 px for the mark.
- Use the reverse lockup on evergreen or photography with sufficient contrast.
- Use the one-color lockup for engraving, embossing, stamps, and single-ink print.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, recolor individual components, add shadows, or place the mark on visually busy backgrounds.

## Accessibility

Evergreen on ivory and ivory on evergreen exceed normal-text contrast requirements. Copper should be used for large display text, borders, or icons; use dark copper `#A64D2C` when accent-colored body text is necessary.
